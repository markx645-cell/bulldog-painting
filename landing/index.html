FAQ

Painters in Cincinnati, OH

The questions we get asked most, answered straight.

Ask every bidder to itemise prep, primer and coat count as separate lines. Quotes that cannot be broken out that way are a number, not a scope — and prep is where the difference between a five-year and a fifteen-year finish lives.

It depends far more on condition than on square footage. Two identical houses can differ by thousands when one needs a day and a half of scraping and priming first. We quote from a site visit, never over the phone.

Protection, surface repair, caulking, priming, the product line, the number of coats, and labour — each on its own line. You can see exactly what you are paying for and what could be trimmed.

A three-bedroom interior with walls, ceilings and trim usually runs four to six working days with a crew of three. Cabinets, wallpaper removal or significant drywall repair add to that, and the day count is on your written estimate.

We move and wrap what we can safely handle. Please clear breakables, electronics and anything irreplaceable before day one — we do not disconnect or move TVs and computers.

Wash, scrape, sand, fill, caulk and spot-prime, with the specific primer named on your quote — bonding over chalked aluminium, stain-blocking over cedar and water marks. Nine out of ten coating failures start here.

Yes. Doors and drawer fronts go to our shop to be sprayed flat; boxes and face frames are sprayed in place behind plastic containment with negative air. Your sink and appliances stay usable throughout.

Roughly mid-April through late October. Modern acrylics can go down to 35°F, but overnight lows matter more than daytime highs — paint that has not formed a film before the temperature drops will fail. We schedule to the forecast, not the calendar.

Yes — written workmanship support on most services, handed to you at the final walkthrough. Peeling or flaking traced to our prep or application gets put right at our cost.

We walk the whole job with you, you point at anything you are not happy with, and we fix it before we invoice. The warranty paperwork is handed over on that same visit.
